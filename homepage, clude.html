Our Heritage

Where craft meets intentional living

Sweet Haven began in a small workshop in Thamel, Kathmandu — where our founder, surrounded by hand-thrown ceramics and ink-dyed textiles, asked a simple question: why should beautiful homes be reserved for a few?

Every piece in our collection is sourced from Nepal's finest artisan communities — weavers in Patan, potters in Bhaktapur, and botanical print-makers who have spent generations perfecting their craft. We do not mass-produce. We curate, with intention.
